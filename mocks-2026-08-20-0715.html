Frequently asked questions

Pricing, students, privacy, and everything else coaches ask before getting started.

The basics

What sport does this work for?

Basketball today. Other sports are coming, but if you coach something else, Reps isn't built for you yet.

Do I have to build out all the drills myself?

No. It comes preloaded with a full basketball library — shooting, ballhandling, conditioning, all ready to assign right away. Want something specific to your program? You can add your own too.

Do I need to already be tracking things some other way?

No. Most coaches get by however they can — a text, a note, a printed page. Reps replaces that with one place.

How much time does this add to my week?

A few minutes to assign work. After that it runs itself — students log on their own, you check in when you want to.

Pricing

Is it actually free, what's the catch?

No catch. Free covers up to 3 students, forever.

What happens when I add a 4th student?

You're prompted to upgrade — $10/month, up to 30 students. See pricing.

Do I need a card to start?

No. Try it free and see for yourself if it's actually useful — nothing to enter, nothing to cancel if it's not for you.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime from your profile. You'll keep everything you already paid for through the end of that period — it just won't renew after.

Your students

Do they need to download anything?

No. They get a text with a link. Tapping it opens their assignment right there — no app to install, nothing to set up first.

What do they have to share?

Just a phone number. No account, no email.

Will it feel like extra homework, more friction?

No. When you assign something, they get a text, tap it, and log what they did — that's the whole process. No new app, no separate login to remember.

Can students message me back?

No chat here. But when they log, they leave you a real note — you see how it went, not just a checkbox. Coaching still happens in person; this keeps that thread alive in between sessions.

What if a student doesn't have their own phone?

A parent's phone works, same link.

Privacy and trust

Who sees my roster?

Only you. Students only see their own assignments.

What happens to my data if I stop using Reps?

It stays yours — nothing is deleted without you asking.

Do you sell or share data?

No, never sold. The vendors we use to run Reps are listed in the privacy policy.

A few more things

Can other coaches or trainers share my account?

Not yet — one account per person today.

Does this replace texting my students?

No — just the homework-assignment part. You'll still text however you already do.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes — it's just a link, works on any phone.

Have an idea, or notice something missing? Let us know at hello@assignreps.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Reps is a small product. Here's exactly what we collect and why.

What we collect

We collect your name, email address, and any student or parent phone numbers you add. We store the practice assignments you create, the rep logs your students submit, and the short note a student can leave with a log. If you subscribe, we store your Stripe customer and subscription IDs and whether your plan is active. Card details go straight to Stripe — we never see or store them.

How we use it

To send you a sign-in code. To send your students their assignment link via SMS. To show you your students' progress. SMS goes to whichever phone number you add for each student — theirs or a parent's, whichever makes sense for that relationship. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Message and data rates may apply.

SMS consent

Before a coach adds any student or parent phone number to Reps, they must obtain verbal consent. The recipient must agree to receive SMS messages before their number is entered. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages — this happens automatically through our messaging provider, not something written into every text. Message and data rates may apply.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We use Supabase (database), Twilio (SMS), Resend (email), and Stripe (payments) to operate the product.

Students and minors

If your child uses Reps, here's what that means.

They don't have an account. A coach adds their name and one phone number — theirs or a parent's — after getting permission in person. We store the practice they're assigned, what they log, and any note they write with it.

Parents: email hello@assignreps.com to have your child's data removed. You don't need to go through the coach.

Deleting your data

Email hello@assignreps.com and we'll remove your account and everything in it. We do this by hand, so give us a few days.

Removing a student from your roster deletes their assignments and logs too. That can't be undone.

Deactivating a student is different: it pauses them and keeps everything. Going over your plan's limit only changes what you can do in the app — it never deletes anything.

Terms of Service

Last updated: August 17, 2026

What Reps is

Reps is a tool for coaches and trainers to assign practice homework, and for students to log their progress.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for obtaining explicit verbal consent before adding any student or parent phone number to Reps.

Before entering a phone number you must confirm with the recipient: “I'd like to send your practice assignments through Reps. You'll get a text with a link to view and log your work. Is that okay?”

You agree not to add phone numbers of anyone who has not verbally agreed to receive messages. Recipients can reply STOP at any time to opt out.

Payments

Reps offers a free tier. Paid plans are billed monthly and can be cancelled anytime. If you have more active students than your plan allows, you can't assign new work until you're back within it.

Availability

We do our best to keep Reps running reliably. We're not liable for outages or data loss.

Termination

We can suspend accounts that violate these terms.

Questions?

Email hello@assignreps.com.
